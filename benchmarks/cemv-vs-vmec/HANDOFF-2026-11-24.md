# MILCA equilibrium benchmark handoff, 24 November 2026

Both MILCA formulations beat stock VMEC2000 at eight cores on the quasi-axisymmetric vacuum case and the 33-surface Solov’ev target. The next step is to read the submitted resolution controls and decide where to refine. stellarator has a concentrated wall error. The li383 case also needs an interior correction.

MILCA’s forward formulation maps flux labels to physical space. Its CEMV formulation maps physical space to flux labels. Both impose fixed-wall, nested-surface ideal magnetohydrodynamic balance with the same pressure and rotational transform profiles as stock Variational Moments Equilibrium Code (VMEC). The independent comparator is MIT-licensed [VMEC2000](https://github.com/ORNL-Fusion/VMEC2000/tree/4e1faff9d4aafa2d941df3dc7b21672d22792c51). See the [benchmark entry points](README.md).

The table compares degree-five CEMV and the finest completed stock reconstruction on the same physical points. The force residual is the normalized RMS of $(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\times\mathbf B-\mu_0\nabla p$, where $\mathbf B$ is magnetic field, $p$ is pressure and $\mu_0$ is the vacuum permeability. The stock curl column uses the curl of its contravariant field reconstruction.

- Increasing the stock angular mode limits reduced the half-flux distances from 1.985 to 0.3884 mm and from 0.8683 to 0.5004 mm, respectively. Hold radial resolution and stopping tolerance fixed while separating mode extent from integration-grid effects.
- The first 0.0625 of the wall-normal spline-block parameter contains 95.0% and 98.4% of the respective outer-band squared force. Most of that band’s squared force is away from tangential block seams. With the interior held fixed, stellarator needs 46.5% lower outer-band RMS force to reach the stock curl value. The li383 interior contribution alone, normalized over the whole window, is 0.01712.
- Two weak negative curvatures persist in the scaled, constrained stellarator Hessian and weaken on degree refinement. They move from approximately $-3.08\times10^{-7}$ and $-1.18\times10^{-9}$ at degree four to $-7.73\times10^{-8}$ and $-1.22\times10^{-10}$ at degree five. Generated analytic directional derivatives show negligible alignment of their flux-label and field-strength responses with the stock discrepancy. Their physical interpretation remains open. The attempted 10% pressure increase stopped above the stationarity tolerance.
